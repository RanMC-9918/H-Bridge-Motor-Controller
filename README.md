## Parts
- Arduino with 4 PWM pins
- Breadboard & Jumper Cables
- 4 NPN transistors (Code assumes all 4 transistors are NPN even though 2 NPN and 2 PNP is more efficient)
- Any motor
- Any motor compatible power supply

## Safety
Do not use transistors not rated for motor output, for example 60V TIP transistors will not survive a 120V motor just because they are in series in the H-Bridge. 
Do not use large motors without a flyback diode, transistors may fry themselves.
